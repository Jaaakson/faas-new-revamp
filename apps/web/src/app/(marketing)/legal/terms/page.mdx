# Terms of Service

**Last updated:** 31 July 2026

## 1. The service

Fvas provides face verification over an API: you enroll a subject's face, and we
compare a later image against it and return a decision with supporting evidence.

## 2. Your obligations

You are the data controller for every subject you enroll. That carries specific
duties, and they are yours rather than ours.

**You must:**

- Obtain informed, explicit consent from every person whose face you enroll,
  before enrolling them.
- Tell them clearly that biometric verification is being used and why.
- Offer a non-biometric alternative where the law requires one.
- Keep a consent record and reference it on the subject.
- Delete subjects when consent is withdrawn or the purpose has ended.
- Secure your API keys, and rotate them if exposed.

**You must not:**

- Enroll anyone without their knowledge or consent.
- Use Fvas for surveillance, covert identification, or tracking people who have
  not consented.
- Use it to profile or discriminate on race, ethnicity, religion, or any
  protected characteristic.
- Enroll minors without verified guardian consent where the law requires it.
- Attempt to reverse-engineer templates or extract our models.
- Resell raw API access without a written agreement.

Violating the "must not" list terminates your account without refund. This is not
boilerplate: biometric misuse is the failure mode that would harm people, and it
is the one we will act on.

## 3. What this service is not

Stated plainly, because a misunderstanding here has real consequences.

- **Not certified presentation-attack detection.** Fvas ships quality gates,
  screen-replay heuristics, and an optional active challenge. It is not certified
  under ISO/IEC 30107-3 and can be defeated by a determined attacker with a
  prepared video or high-quality artefact.
- **Not suitable as a sole control for unsupervised remote onboarding** against a
  motivated adversary. It is designed as a step-up factor within a session you
  already control.
- **Not a complete KYC or identity-proofing solution.** No document verification,
  no watchlist screening.
- **Not infallible.** Face recognition has a non-zero false accept and false
  reject rate, and performance varies across demographic groups. You must design
  a fallback path for legitimate users who fail verification.

You are responsible for deciding whether these properties are acceptable for your
use case, and for the consequences of that decision.
