# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 31 July 2026

## 1. Roles

Fvas processes personal data in two distinct capacities, and the distinction
determines who is responsible for what.

**As a controller**, for our own customers: the people who create a Fvas account,
log into the dashboard, and are billed. We decide how that data is used.

**As a processor**, for biometric data: the faces of *your* end users, which we
process only on your instruction. You are the controller. You obtain consent, you
determine the retention period, and you decide when data is deleted.

## 2. What we collect

### Account data (as controller)

| Data | Purpose | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, email, password hash | Authenticate you | Contract |
| Organization name, plan | Provide and bill the service | Contract |
| IP address, user agent, login events | Security and abuse prevention | Legitimate interest |
| Usage counts | Billing and quota enforcement | Contract |

### Biometric data (as processor)

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| **Face templates** — 512-dimension numerical vectors | Until you delete the subject |
| Quality metrics — sharpness, brightness, pose | Per your log retention setting |
| Verification records — decision, score, threshold, timestamp | Per your log retention setting |
| **Raw images** | **Discarded immediately after processing**, unless you explicitly enable retention |

Image retention is off by default and must be enabled deliberately per
organization.

## 3. What a template is

A face template is a vector of 512 numbers produced by a neural network. It is
not a photograph and cannot be viewed as one. It is bound to a specific model
version and is meaningless outside it.

We nevertheless treat templates as **sensitive personal data** in every respect.
Research into partially reconstructing facial imagery from embeddings exists, and
"difficult to invert" is not the same as "safe". Access controls, encryption, and
deletion guarantees are applied on the assumption that a template identifies a
person — because it does.

## 4. What we never do

- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not train models on customer data. There is no training pipeline.
- We do not share biometric data with third parties.
- We do not use biometric data for any purpose other than the verification you
  requested and same-tenant duplicate detection.
- We do not log image bytes, templates, tokens, or API key secrets. A redaction
  test in our build asserts this.

## 5. Security

- TLS 1.3 in transit; HSTS with preload.
- AES-256 encryption at rest on database volumes and backups.
- Passwords hashed with Argon2id; API keys stored as SHA-256 hashes.
- Least-privilege database access. No standing human access to production data;
  break-glass access is time-boxed, approved, and logged.
- Append-only audit logging of every mutating action.
- Tenant isolation enforced at the data-access layer and verified by automated
  cross-tenant tests on every endpoint.

Full technical detail is published in our public repository rather than
summarised here, so it can be independently checked.

## 6. Deletion

Deleting a subject **physically removes** its template rows. It is not a flag — a
soft-deleted biometric template is still retained biometric data, which defeats
the purpose of the request.

| Data | On deletion |
| --- | --- |
| Face templates | Removed immediately |
| Retained images, if enabled | Removed immediately |
| Verification history | Retained for audit, with biometric fields removed |
| Backups | Rotated out within 30 days |

Closing your account removes all associated biometric data within 30 days.

## 7. International transfers and residency

Data is processed in the region configured for your organization. The default is
`ap-southeast`. Cross-region transfer does not happen without an explicit
configuration change by you.

## 8. Your rights

Under GDPR, UU PDP No. 27/2022, and comparable regimes, individuals may request
access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection.

If you are an **end user** of a company that uses Fvas, contact that company —
they are the controller and hold your consent record. We will support them in
responding, but we cannot act on your data without their instruction.

If you are a **Fvas account holder**, email hello@fvas.io.

## 9. Sub-processors

We use infrastructure providers for hosting, database, and email delivery. A
current list is available on request, and we will give notice before adding a
sub-processor that handles biometric data.

## 10. Changes

Material changes will be announced by email and in the changelog before taking
effect.
