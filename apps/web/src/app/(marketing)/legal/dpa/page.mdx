# Data Processing Addendum

**Last updated:** 31 July 2026

This addendum governs our processing of personal data on your behalf. Where it
conflicts with the Terms of Service, this document controls for data processing.

## 1. Roles

You are the **controller**. Fvas is the **processor**. We process personal data
only on your documented instructions, which are given through your use of the
API and your organization settings.

## 2. Scope of processing

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Subject matter** | Face verification |
| **Duration** | For as long as your account is active |
| **Nature** | Template extraction, storage, comparison, and logging |
| **Purpose** | Verifying that a person matches a previously enrolled subject |
| **Data types** | Biometric templates, quality metrics, verification records, and — only if you enable retention — facial images |
| **Data subjects** | The end users you choose to enroll |

Special-category data under GDPR Article 9 and specific personal data under UU
PDP No. 27/2022 is in scope. Establishing a lawful basis for it is your
responsibility as controller.

## 3. Our obligations

We will:

- Process personal data only on your documented instructions.
- Notify you if an instruction appears to breach applicable law.
- Ensure personnel with access are bound by confidentiality.
- Implement the technical and organisational measures in section 4.
- Assist you with data subject requests, DPIAs, and regulator enquiries.
- Delete or return personal data on termination, at your choice.
- Make available the information needed to demonstrate compliance, and allow
  audits under reasonable notice.

## 4. Security measures

| Area | Measure |
| --- | --- |
| Encryption in transit | TLS 1.3 minimum, HSTS with preload |
| Encryption at rest | AES-256 on database volumes and backups |
| Credentials | Argon2id for passwords, SHA-256 for API keys |
| Access control | Role-based, least privilege, no standing production access; break-glass is time-boxed, approved, and logged |
| Tenant isolation | Enforced at the data-access layer, verified by automated cross-tenant tests on every endpoint |
| Data minimisation | Templates only; raw images discarded unless you enable retention |
| Logging | Append-only audit log; secrets, templates, and image bytes are never logged |
| Availability | Encrypted nightly backups, point-in-time recovery, tested restores |
| Development | Peer-reviewed changes, dependency and secret scanning in CI |

## 5. Sub-processors

You authorise us to use sub-processors for infrastructure, database hosting, and
transactional email. A current list is available on request.

We remain liable for their performance, impose equivalent obligations on them by
contract, and will give at least 30 days' notice before adding a sub-processor
that handles biometric data — during which you may object and terminate without
penalty.

## 6. International transfers

Data is processed in the region configured for your organization; the default is
`ap-southeast`. Transfers outside that region require your instruction and are
made under an appropriate transfer mechanism.

Data residency guarantees, including single-region and on-premise deployment, are
available on Enterprise.

## 7. Breach notification

We will notify you **without undue delay and within 72 hours** of becoming aware
of a personal data breach, with the nature of the breach, categories and
approximate number of affected records, likely consequences, and the measures
taken.

**Any exposure of biometric templates is treated as maximum severity by default.**
A password can be changed; a face cannot. Our incident process assumes this
without requiring a judgement call under pressure.

## 8. Data subject requests

If we receive a request directly from one of your end users, we will not respond
substantively. We will forward it to you within 5 business days, since you hold
the consent record and the relationship.

We support access, deletion, and portability through the API:

- **Access** — retrieve the subject and its verification history
- **Deletion** — `DELETE /v1/subjects/{id}` physically removes templates
- **Portability** — export verification history as CSV or JSON

## 9. Deletion and return

On termination you may export your data for 30 days. After that, all personal
data is deleted from live systems immediately and from backups within the 30-day
backup rotation.

Deleting a subject removes its templates immediately. Verification history is
retained for audit with biometric fields removed, unless you request full
erasure.

## 10. Audits

You may audit compliance once per 12 months with 30 days' notice, or more often
if a regulator requires it. We will respond to reasonable security
questionnaires.

We are not currently SOC 2 certified. Controls are being built to be auditable,
and we will say so rather than imply a certification we do not hold.
